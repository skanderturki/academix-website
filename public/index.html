From course assessment to the ABET Self-Study Report

Academix is the accreditation platform for ABET programs. Instructors assess, coordinators report, quality managers track readiness criterion by criterion, and the Self-Study Report is generated from the evidence you collected all year.

The platform

  • Self-Study Report, generated: your commission's official outline, Criteria 1 to 8, Program Criteria and appendices, with Tables 5-1 and 6-1 and an evidence binder, as an editable Word document.
  • Program Criteria, word for word: the 2026-27 Program Criteria for every EAC, ETAC and CAC discipline.
  • ABET Readiness Center: where each criterion stands and what is missing next.
  • Student Outcomes assessment: PEOs, Student Outcomes, performance indicators and CLOs in one chain, with assessment campaigns per term.
  • Course reports: lock on submit, with Word export in the ETEC TP-154 format.
  • Continuous improvement: constituent surveys, PEO reviews and closed-loop improvement actions for Criterion 4.
